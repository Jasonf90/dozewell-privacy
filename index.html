Dozewell Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

The short version: Dozewell doesn't collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you log stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, and it reaches only the people you invite.

What Dozewell stores, and where

  • What you enter. Your child's name, birth date and due date; feeds, sleep, diaper changes, baths and tummy time; journal entries and notes; and any photos you choose to add. Dozewell keeps this in its private storage on your device.
  • Widgets and Live Activities. Dozewell shares a small summary, such as a running timer, with its own widgets and Live Activities on the same device. It doesn't leave the device.
  • Imports. When you import a file exported from another app, Dozewell reads only the file you choose, on your device, and adds its entries to your log like any other. The file itself isn't kept or sent anywhere.
  • Photos. Dozewell uses the system photo picker, so it only receives the photos you pick. It can't see the rest of your library.
  • iCloud. When you're signed in to iCloud, Dozewell syncs your household between your devices through your own private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit. Your logs are end-to-end encrypted, your photos are encrypted too, and the developer can see neither. They count toward your iCloud storage. Without iCloud, everything stays on the device it was logged on.

Sharing with a co-parent

  • Invitations. When you invite someone from the Family tab, Apple's CloudKit shares your household with the Apple Account you choose. Only people you invite can join. The invitation goes out the way you pick, such as Messages or Mail.
  • What they see. People who join see and add to the household's children, logs, running timers, journal with its photos, and playbook. Apple shows the people in a household each other's names, or the email address or phone number an invitation went to. Reminders and notification choices stay on each person's own device.
  • Where it lives. The shared household stays in the inviting person's iCloud and counts toward their storage.
  • Leaving and stopping. Someone who joined can leave at any time, and the person who invited them can stop sharing. Either way, each device keeps a copy of what it already has, and new records stop reaching the other person.

What Dozewell doesn't do

  • No Dozewell account or sign-in. Sync uses the Apple Account you're already signed in to.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software from anyone.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No selling of data, and no sharing with anyone you didn't invite. The developer runs no servers and receives nothing you log.

Reminders

Reminders, including the optional note the day before a free week ends, are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you use Siri or the Shortcuts app with Dozewell, Apple processes your request under Apple's Privacy Policy. Dozewell receives only the action, such as "log a wet diaper", and stores it like any other log.

Subscriptions

Dozewell Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the purchase, and the developer never sees your name, email address or payment details. The app checks with Apple's StoreKit, on your device, whether your household has an active subscription. When you share a household, the date your subscription ends travels with your name to the people in it, so they're covered too; it's end-to-end encrypted like your logs, and the developer can't see it. You can manage or cancel it any time in your Apple Account settings.

Your choices

  • View and export. Everything you've logged stays viewable, and you can export it from Settings, with or without a subscription.
  • Delete. "Delete all data" in Settings removes everything Dozewell has stored on the device, and the household in your iCloud. If you joined someone else's household, you leave it first, and it stays with the person who invited you. Deleting the app removes what is on that device; your iCloud copy stays until you delete it in Settings or in your iCloud storage settings.

Children

Parents and caregivers use Dozewell to keep track of their own child's day. The app isn't directed to children, and the developer doesn't collect information from or about anyone.
