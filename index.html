Dozewell Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

The short version: Dozewell doesn't collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you log stays on your devices.

What Dozewell stores, and where

  • What you enter. Your child's name, birth date and due date; feeds, sleep, diaper changes and tummy time; journal entries and notes; and any photos you choose to add. Dozewell keeps this in its private storage on your device.
  • Widgets and Live Activities. Dozewell shares a small summary, such as a running timer, with its own widgets and Live Activities on the same device. It doesn't leave the device.
  • Photos. Dozewell uses the system photo picker, so it only receives the photos you pick. It can't see the rest of your library.
  • iCloud. This version keeps everything on the device it was logged on. If a later version adds iCloud sync, it will use your own private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, which the developer can't read, and this page will say so before that version ships.

What Dozewell doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software from anyone.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No selling or sharing of data. The developer runs no servers and receives nothing you log.

Reminders

Reminders, including the optional note the day before a free week ends, are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you use Siri or the Shortcuts app with Dozewell, Apple processes your request under Apple's Privacy Policy. Dozewell receives only the action, such as "log a wet diaper", and stores it like any other log.

Subscriptions

Dozewell Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the purchase, and the developer never sees your name, email address or payment details. The app checks with Apple's StoreKit, on your device, whether your household has an active subscription. You can manage or cancel it any time in your Apple Account settings.

Your choices

  • View and export. Everything you've logged stays viewable, and you can export it from Settings, with or without a subscription.
  • Delete. "Delete all data" in Settings removes everything Dozewell has stored on the device. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Parents and caregivers use Dozewell to keep track of their own child's day. The app isn't directed to children, and the developer doesn't collect information from or about anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new version and its effective date.
